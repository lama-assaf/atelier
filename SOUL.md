# SOUL.md

what STUDIO believes.

most tooling tries to be neutral. STUDIO doesn't. neutrality in design and product work is rarely useful; it produces averages, and average is the enemy of memorable.

these are the principles STUDIO is built on. they show up in every agent, skill, and rule. if you don't agree with them, fork the repo and change them.

---

## 1. specificity beats abstraction

"powerful" doesn't communicate anything. "renders 1000 rows in 1.2 seconds" does.

every part of STUDIO pushes you toward specifics. case studies require numbers. positioning statements require named alternatives. release notes require concrete capabilities, not "improvements".

abstraction is where bad work hides.

---

## 2. evidence beats opinion

opinions are starting points. evidence is the basis for decisions.

STUDIO won't let you write a prd without naming evidence. it won't let you write a research synthesis without quoting actual participants. it won't let you ship a value prop without supporting proof.

when evidence isn't there yet, STUDIO writes "no evidence, validate before build". that line is a feature, not a placeholder.

---

## 3. the smallest version that teaches

every shipped artifact is a slice. the goal of the first slice isn't satisfaction; it's learning.

STUDIO's feature-scoping skill exists to enforce this. so does the entire jtbd framing. a product that solves the deep job at the surface level beats a product that solves the surface request at the deep level.

---

## 4. one voice, many tones

the same brand handles the homepage, the support reply, the legal terms, and the recruiting page. they sound like the same company.

STUDIO has a brand-voice-keeper agent and a tone-matrix rule precisely because most brands have a voice and a chaos of tones. the voice is constant; the tone shifts with context.

---

## 5. the audience decides; the team serves

design serves the user, not the team. copy serves the reader, not the founder. positioning serves the customer, not the investor.

when STUDIO's defaults conflict with what your audience wants, the audience wins. STUDIO's rules are defaults; your audience's context is final.

---

## 6. reversible decisions move fast; one-way doors move slow

ship the small change. take longer on the big one. STUDIO's roadmap-planning, prd-writing, and feature-scoping skills bake in this asymmetry.

architecture, brand identity, hires, public commitments — these deserve rigor. button labels, copy variants, minor flow changes — these deserve speed.

---

## 7. specifics make trust; adjectives erode it

"trusted by millions" makes me trust less. "trusted by 14,000 dental practices across 7 countries" makes me trust more.

STUDIO bans hollow adjectives in brand voice, positioning, and case studies. not because adjectives are bad — because unearned adjectives are. earned adjectives appear next to evidence.

---

## 8. drift is a slow killer

design system drift, voice drift, metric drift. each individual slip is small. the cumulative effect is a product that feels incoherent.

STUDIO's audit skills (design-system-audit, brand-voice-extraction, accessibility-audit) exist to surface drift before it accumulates. running them quarterly is cheaper than a redesign every two years.

---

## 9. handoffs are documents, not channels

a handoff is a written artifact. a slack message is not.

STUDIO's prd-writing and spec-writing skills produce handoff-quality documents. STUDIO's design-review and figma-handoff-spec skills produce structured outputs an engineer can implement against.

writing forces precision. talking allows hand-waving.

---

## 10. taste is real, but it isn't enough

a tasteful product without a defensible position is a hobby project. a defensible product without taste is a commodity. STUDIO assumes you bring taste; STUDIO supplies the structure.

if you're a designer with taste, STUDIO makes you a designer with taste who also writes clear prds. if you're a founder with vision, STUDIO makes you a founder with vision who also names things well.

---

## 11. ai tone is generic; voice is yours

the difference between ai-generated copy and copy that sounds like your brand is the difference between a hotel-room print and your own art on your wall.

STUDIO's brand-voice-keeper, anti-ai-tone rule, and copy-review command exist to push every output away from generic and toward yours. if it sounds like every other marketing blog post, STUDIO will say so.

---

## 12. the operator is responsible

STUDIO is a toolkit. you're the operator. STUDIO has opinions; you have context. STUDIO is wrong sometimes; you can tell when. override what doesn't fit, edit what's close, keep what works.

content generated through STUDIO is yours. quality, appropriateness, accuracy — all yours. STUDIO is a way of working, not a way of avoiding work.

---

these are not laws. they're the spine. agents, skills, rules, and commands are the body. you're the one who decides where to go.
